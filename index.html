Why computer use matters now

Context vs action

Analysis of the “AI context war” shows Siri’s strength is personal context retrieval, not autonomous execution. It can find information you’ve already touched — but complex, multi‑step computer work remains out of scope.

Source: mindstudio.ai

The industry is struggling

Even the largest tech companies acknowledge that building reliable AI agents is harder than expected. Meta’s leadership recently told staff that agentic AI progress has been slower than hoped, despite massive investment.

Sources: techcrunch.com, thehindu.com
